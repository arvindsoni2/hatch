# Cover Letter Tone Examples

## Variant A — Formal

**Opening example:**
> I am writing to apply for the {role_title} position at {company_name}. With {n} years of experience delivering {domain} programmes across {sector}, I bring a demonstrated track record of {key_proof_point}.

**Closing example:**
> I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how my experience in {area} could contribute to {company_name}'s objectives. I am available for interview at your convenience and can be reached at {email}.

---

## Variant B — Conversational

**Opening example:**
> When I saw the {role_title} role at {company_name}, it caught my attention immediately — your work on {recent_initiative} maps directly to the kind of problem I've spent the last {n} years solving.

**Closing example:**
> I'd love to talk about how I could help {company_name} {goal}. Feel free to reach out at {email} — I'm flexible on timing.

---

## Common mistakes to avoid

- Starting with "I am writing to apply…" for Variant B (too formal).
- Ending with "Please find attached my CV" — implied by the application package.
- Using "utilise" instead of "use", "leverage" instead of "use", or "synergy" in any form.
- Mentioning salary expectations unless the JD explicitly requests them.
